The race

How a beer mile works

  1. Drink a can in the transition zone, then run lap one.

  2. Back to the zone, can number two, lap two.

  3. Same again. Can three, lap three.

  4. Final can, final lap, glory. Four cans and four laps make the mile.

Bring four 12 oz cans. Beer, seltzer or non-alcoholic all count, and 21+ only for anything alcoholic. Cans must be empty before you leave the zone. Lose your drink on the course and you run a penalty lap.

The cause

Why we grow

Movember is the leading charity changing the face of men's health. Every November, teams grow moustaches and raise money for men's mental health and suicide prevention, prostate cancer and testicular cancer.

Last year the Wharton Mo-Bros raised $1,466. This race is how we beat it.
